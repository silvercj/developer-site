The UK duty on the liquid you use

From 1 October 2026 the UK charges Vaping Products Duty of £2.20 on every 10ml of vaping liquid, with or without nicotine. Hardware is not covered.

ml

A standard bottle is 10ml. A prefilled pod is usually 2ml.

£2.20duty on that liquid, a week
£114.40duty on that liquid, a year

That is the duty itself, and it is the only certain figure. It is charged to the business that makes or imports the liquid. How much of it reaches the shelf price is each shop's decision. If a shop passed all of it on, VAT would apply on top: £2.64 a week, £137.28 a year. That line is an assumption, not a forecast. Check your own receipts after October.

The bigger number

The duty is the part in the news. The first figure on this page is the larger one, and it was there before any tax: what you already spend. Cutting down shrinks it a little every week. Stopping ends it.

OneLess estimates what you keep as you cut down or stop, from your own weekly spend. During a taper it counts only the days you confirm you logged in full, so the figure stays honest. It is free on the App Store, with an optional Premium part for a weekly review and one next step.
