OneLess for iPhone

Make space between you
and the next vape.

Log each pick-up and watch the gap grow. When a craving hits, breathing, a distraction or your own reason is one tap away. Nothing you enter leaves your phone.

Coming to the App StoreSupport

Three ways in

1

Taper to a quit date

Wait a little longer between vapes each week. The app suggests the gap; you practise it. Two, four or eight weeks.

2

Stop on a date

No reduction first. Pick a day, get ready, then stop. Home counts down and tells you what happens on the day.

3

Already stopped

Tell it when. It tracks your time free, earned milestones and estimated savings from that moment.

Free and Premium

A clear plan to stop. Support to stay stopped.

Free, always

  • The timer and pick-up logging, and recording a stop date or a slip.
  • Your streak, earned milestones and estimated savings, with a goal to put them towards. Slips never erase them.
  • Craving support: breathing, distraction and your own reason in large type.
  • Your logs, your history and one complete review observation.
  • Any plan you write stays readable, whatever happens with Premium.

OneLess Premium

  • Before you stop: today's gap target on Home, a gap that grows each week, and preparation steps before the day.
  • After you stop: optional check-ins, a one-line plan for each difficult situation, and a review of what you reported and logged.
  • The full review with trigger insights, more than one savings goal at a time, and CSV export.

Monthly, annual with a free trial, or lifetime. Prices are shown in the app. If Premium ends you keep everything you made.

Private by design

No account

Nothing to sign up for. The app works the moment it opens.

On your phone

Every entry is stored on the device. We cannot see it. Read the privacy notice.

Not a medical programme

OneLess is a structure to practise. For help stopping, the NHS has free support.
