OneLess

Make space between you and the next vape.

Home › OneLess

OneLess is an iPhone app for people who want to vape less, or stop. Pick a plan, log each pick-up, and let the timer show you how long until the week's gap is met. When a craving hits, breathing, a distraction, or your own reason is one tap away.

Three plans

  • Taper to a quit date. Wait a little longer between vapes each week. We suggest the gap; you practise it.
  • Stop on a date. A short checklist to get ready, then stop.
  • Already stopped. Track your time free, milestones and estimated savings from the day you stopped.

Private by design

Everything you enter stays on your phone. There is no account and no sign-in. See the privacy notice.

Free and Premium

The plans, timer, logging, craving support, earned milestones and savings are free. OneLess Premium adds a full progress review built only from what you log, gap and trigger insights, and CSV export.

OneLess is a structure to practise, not a medical programme. If you want help stopping, the NHS has free support.
