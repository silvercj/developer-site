Common questions

I slipped. Have I lost everything? No. Your savings estimate and earned milestones stay. The current stretch restarts from the slip.

Can I change plan? Yes: Settings › Change plan. Your log and history are kept. Nothing changes until you save.

Where is my data? On your phone only. Settings › Delete all data removes it.

Premium stopped working. Settings › Restore purchases. If a subscription has lapsed, the free features and all your history remain.

Refunds and subscription management are handled by Apple: manage subscriptions · request a refund.

Health

OneLess is a structure to practise, not a medical programme. For help stopping, the NHS quit-vaping guidance is free.
