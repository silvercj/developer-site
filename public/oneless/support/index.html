When you write

Include your iPhone model and iOS version if it is about a bug. Never include anything you logged in the app unless you want to.

Common questions

I slipped. Have I lost everything?
No. Your savings estimate and earned milestones stay. The current stretch restarts from the slip.
Can I change plan?
Yes: Settings › Change plan. Your log and history are kept. Nothing changes until you save.
Where is my data?
On your phone only. Settings › Delete all data removes it.
Premium stopped working.
Settings › Restore purchases. If a subscription has lapsed, the free features, your history and any plans you wrote all remain.
Refunds and subscription management
Handled by Apple: manage subscriptions · request a refund.

Health

OneLess is a structure to practise, not a medical programme. For help stopping, the NHS quit-vaping guidance is free.
