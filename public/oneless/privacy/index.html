Who is responsible

The app: everything stays on your phone

OneLess has no account and no sign-in. What you enter in the app is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • Your plan: the kind of plan, its dates, and how many times a day you said you pick up your vape.
  • Your first name and the reason you gave for stopping, if you entered them.
  • Your weekly spend figure, used only to estimate savings.
  • Pick-ups and slips you log, cravings you log with the trigger you chose, and the outcome.
  • Checklist progress, which support technique you last used, and the plans you have moved on from.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else by the app. We cannot see it. It is included in your device backup if you back up your phone, under Apple's terms.

Deleting it. Settings › Delete all data removes everything above from the device. Deleting the app also removes it.

Export. The CSV export, part of Premium, writes your log to a file that you choose where to send. Nothing is sent by us.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled on your device by the app. No notification content leaves your phone.

Purchases

OneLess Premium is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and holds the purchase record; we receive no name, email or payment details. The app checks your entitlement with Apple to unlock Premium and to switch it off if a subscription lapses or is refunded. Apple's handling of your data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Analytics

The current version of the app contains no analytics or crash-reporting library and sends no usage data to us. If a future version adds anonymous usage measurement, this section will say exactly what is collected, what is never collected, and how to turn it off, before that version ships.

This website

This site is static and is served by Cloudflare. Cloudflare records standard request logs (such as IP address, user agent and the page requested) to serve and protect the site, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Support email

If you email us, we receive your address and what you wrote. Mail to PLACEHOLDER_SUPPORT_EMAIL is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to a mailbox we control, where it is kept for as long as needed to help you, and deleted afterwards. We do not add you to any list.

Your rights

Because the app keeps your data on your device, you already control it: you can view, export (Premium) and delete it yourself. For the support channel, you can ask us to delete your messages at any time. If you are in the UK or EU you also have the right to complain to your data protection regulator.

Children

OneLess is for adults who vape. It is not intended for anyone under 18.
